Part 2

Class by class

Each stat broken out on its own card
Data freshness

Values for these characters may have changed since they were recorded, so a class's highest or median can shift once they update. Age uses the oldest of the three stat sections (General, Advanced, Special).

Physical attack classes: Assassin Cross, Lord Knight, Rebellion, Sniper, Summoner (physical build). Magic attack classes: High Wizard, Professor, Summoner (magic build). Attack classes are read on 12 stats: Ignore M.DEF and M.DMG Bonus are left out for physical classes, Ignore P.DEF and P.DMG Bonus for magic classes. Defence/support classes (Biochemist, High Priest, Gypsy, Minstrel, Summoner support build) are read on the 7 defence stats. Attack stats use attackers only as the baseline (median, percentile and range): the 19 physical attack characters for Ignore P.DEF and P.DMG Bonus, the 14 magic attack characters for Ignore M.DEF and M.DMG Bonus, and all 33 attack characters for Demi-Human DMG, Medium DMG and PVP Bonus. Defence characters do not build these stats and would drag the baseline down; whole-guild medians would be 1,590 (Ignore P.DEF), 439 (Ignore M.DEF), 81.2% (P.DMG Bonus), 94.4% (M.DMG Bonus), 4.9% (Demi-Human DMG), 9.9% (Medium DMG) and 4,005 (PVP Bonus). Defence stats use the whole guild. Summoner is split by build because its builds carry very different stats.

Median = the middle value of the class. Percentile = the share of the baseline with a lower value, so 50 is the middle and 100 the top. raw P.DEF = (P.DEF − 150) ÷ (1 + Equipment P.DEF% ÷ 100); raw M.DEF uses M.DEF and Equipment M.DEF% the same way. DMG Bonus and DMG Reduction are the % values.
